How to make a perfect grilled cheese sandwich

What you'll need!

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 2 slices of cheese (cheddar or American)
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • A non-stick pan
  • A spatula

Steps

  1. Heat the pan over medium heat.
  2. Butter one side of each slice of bread.
  3. Place one slice of bread, butter side down, in the pan.
  4. Add the cheese slices on top of the bread in the pan.Make sure the cheese is centered
  5. Place the second slice of bread on top, butter side up.
  6. Cook until the bottom slice is golden brown, then flip and cook the other side until golden brown and the cheese is melted.
  7. Remove from heat and let cool for a minute before cutting
